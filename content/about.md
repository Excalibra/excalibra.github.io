Following the tides of data flows, I draw near,  
Chasing the signals that surge in waves.  
We grow closer and closer,  
Distance compressed into a moment's delay.  

Hesitating between near and far,  
Unable to see through these ambiguous eyes.  
As if within reach,  
Yet when I approach, it quietly fades away.
